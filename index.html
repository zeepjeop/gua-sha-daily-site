Your photos stay yours

Private by architecture, not by promise

The daily photo exists for one pair of eyes: yours.

Never uploaded

Photos are encrypted (AES-256) on your device and stay in the app's private storage. They never touch a server — there isn't one to send them to.

No scanning, no scores

The app never analyses, measures, or rates your face. The camera guide is a simple overlay. The only judge of your before & after is you.

You hold the keys

No account, no sign-up. Nothing is ever uploaded — a photo only leaves if you tap Share or save a copy yourself. Delete the app, and everything is gone.

FAQ

Fair questions

Is it really private?

Yes — by design, not policy. Photos are AES-256 encrypted on your device, excluded from cloud backups, and there is no server that receives them. No account, no face scanning, ever. Nothing is uploaded — a photo only leaves if you tap Share or save a copy to your own photos.

What happens after the free trial?

Your daily photo, streak, core routine and full 14-day journey stay free forever. The full routine library and unlimited journeys need Plus — priced exactly as shown above, with no hidden trial-to-charge tricks. We never take a payment method for the free trial.

Do you promise results?

No. Gua sha is a calm self-care practice, and we make no claims about what it changes. Nothing is measured or scored. The app gives you two honest photos, two weeks apart — what you see there is yours to judge.

When does it launch?

Remi is in closed testing on Android right now — you can join the test from the button up top, or write to us and we'll walk you through it. iPhone comes later. This page updates the moment it's public.
